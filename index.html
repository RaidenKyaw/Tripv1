The actual problem

Two questions nobody can answer at once

Planners tell you when everyone's free. Flight tools tell you when it's cheap. Neither knows the other, so the group chat stalls at "when's everyone free?" and dies at "that weekend's $480 now."

Freewheel fuses the two. Your shared free weekends, priced from your home airport, ranked cheapest first.

Questions

The ones people actually ask

Do my friends need to sign up?

No. That's deliberate. They tap your link, add their name and their dates, and they're in. Signing up is only for the extras — saved trips, price alerts, your crew rate.

Is this an app?

No, and it won't be. It's a website, so a link in a group chat just works — no install, no app store, no "download this first".

Where do the prices come from?

A nightly cache of return fares from your home airport, built from the Travelpayouts fare data API. We show indicative prices per person and link you out to book — Freewheel never takes your money for a flight or holds a booking.

Can we use dates that aren't weekends?

Yes — add a custom range with a label like "mid-sem break" and everyone can tap that too. Custom ranges are priced off the nearest cached weekend, so they show with a ~.

Do you book anything or take commission from me?

We don't book, and you never pay us for a trip. When you click through to an airline or a hostel, we may earn a small affiliate fee from them at no cost to you. It's a rounding error next to the membership — we'd rather tell you than have you find out.

What if the group never books?

Then the membership wasn't worth it, and you should cancel. The honest pitch is that most of the year nothing happens, and the alerts are what you're paying for in those months.

Which airports work?

Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane at launch. Deal quality depends on how many people fly from the same airport, so we open new ones as the catchment fills rather than all at once.
